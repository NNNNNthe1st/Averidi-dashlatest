# Row-grain recognition (original requirement 82)

Each newly profiled version receives an advisory assessment of the full prepared version, not just its preview. Orders and invoices use separate identities. Order and invoice line items require a complete, unique composite document/line key; line numbers may repeat between documents. Customer, product and transaction candidates require complete unique keys. A daily candidate requires distinct complete calendar dates without a stronger identity. Date uniqueness alone cannot establish daily aggregation: customers must confirm their business timezone and row meaning.

Multiple document identities, ambiguous entity identities, missing/unsafe/repeated keys, missing document identity for lines, and fewer than two rows produce **Undetermined**, with a review explanation. Suggestions include row count, identity field names, confidence and evidence, never raw identifier values. An exact unique key is evidence about cardinality, not proof of a business definition.

Studio shows the persisted assessment beside calculation rules. It does not select a grain, confirm fields or overwrite saved rules. New `INVOICE`, `INVOICE_ITEM`, `ORDER_ITEM` and `DAY` choices receive calculation-time validation. Daily validation uses the confirmed business timezone and rejects multiple instants on the same business day. Invoices never implicitly become orders. No automatic deduplication or document-total allocation occurs.

Line-item revenue, cost, profit, margin and price calculations require explicit confirmation that every monetary input, including refunds and discounts, belongs to the line rather than a document total repeated across lines. Without that choice the results remain unavailable pending confirmation. Equal legitimate line amounts are not silently discarded. Cancellation-row exclusion retains its existing order-only contract; other grains require an appropriate already-excluded source instead of an inferred cancellation policy.

Older versions are not reprofiled or rewritten. Their saved mappings, metric rules and existing calculation behavior remain intact. Old versions without the assessment show an explicit unassessed message. The additional grain/amount scope affects governance hashes only for the new grains; legacy hashes remain unchanged. Analysis engine version 4 remains valid for unchanged legacy semantics; new-grain results are bound to new explicit rule values and semantic revisions.

Verification: `tests/row-grain.test.ts` covers classification, ambiguous and invalid keys, full-version cardinality, collision-safe composite keys, monetary consent, invoice/order separation, daily timezone validation and legacy governance stability. `tests/fixtures/row-grain-browser.ts` exercises real upload/profile, persisted advisory results, explicit UI confirmation and worker calculation, then desktop/mobile accessibility. The completed batch's full-gate result is recorded in the architecture progress log.
